## 2. Full Description (Copy & Paste)

```markdown
Pocket UPSC is an offline-first Civil Services Examination (CSE) preparation engine designed to help aspirants systematically navigate, track, and master the UPSC General Studies curriculum.

🏛️ KEY FEATURES:

1. 📋 COMPREHENSIVE SYLLABUS MATRIX
• Complete GS Paper 1, 2, 3, and 4 curriculum broken down into granular micro-topics.
• Real-time progress auditing with visual percentage completion meters.
• Offline milestone checklists to track read, revised, and mastered subjects.

2. ⚡ PRELIMS QUESTION BANK & TIMED QUIZ ENGINE
• Thousands of high-yield practice questions categorized by subject (Indian Polity, Modern History, Geography, Economy, Environment & Science).
• Real-time exam timer and immediate analytical answer explanations.

3. ✍️ MAINS GS MODEL ANSWER DESK
• Topic-wise descriptive questions with structured administrative model answers.
• High-yield framework templates, bullet-point revisions, and key case law references.

4. 📜 10+ YEARS PRELIMS PYQ VAULT
• Topic-categorized authentic past examination questions.
• Identify historical question weightage and recurring themes across UPSC Prelims.

5. 🔒 100% OFFLINE-READY & PRIVATE
• Fast, local IndexedDB storage. Study anywhere without needing constant internet.
• Clean, ad-free study environment dedicated to deep concentration.

────────────────────────
⚠️ GOVERNMENT AFFILIATION DISCLAIMER:
Pocket UPSC is an independent educational tool developed to assist aspirants in self-study. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, or in any way officially associated with the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) or any government entity.
```
