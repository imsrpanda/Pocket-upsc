Privacy Policy for Pocket UPSC

Last updated: October 17, 2026
Important Disclaimer: Pocket UPSC is an independent educational preparation engine and offline curriculum tracker. It is NOT affiliated with, associated with, authorized by, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) or any government department or agency.

Pocket UPSC ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, store, and safeguard your information when you use the Pocket UPSC mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Pocket UPSC is designed primarily as an offline-first learning application. We minimize data collection to the maximum extent possible:

  • Local Study Data (Stored on Your Device): Your syllabus progress, checklist states, saved notes, bookmarks, and quiz scores are stored locally on your device via client-side storage (IndexedDB). This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.
  • Optional Google Authentication: If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your basic profile information (such as your Name, Email Address, and Profile Picture) provided via Firebase Authentication to personalize your cadet desk and manage sync.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: We do not track you across apps and we do not collect or sell your Google Advertising ID (AAID).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the minimal data collected exclusively to:

  • Display and calculate your progress through UPSC General Studies syllabus modules.
  • Provide local quiz grading, answer explanations, and performance metrics.
  • Authenticate your account if you choose to sign in with Google.

3. Advertising & Tracking

Pocket UPSC does not display third-party advertisements and does not integrate ad networks. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third-party marketing entities.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your local study data is stored securely on your local device. Authentication credentials are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption via Google Firebase Services.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have full control over your study data at all times:

  • You can clear your local database, syllabus ticks, and notes at any time from the in-app Settings Desk > Reset Data.
  • To request full deletion of any account details associated with Google Sign-In, you can email us directly at the contact address below.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for adult students and civil services aspirants. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
